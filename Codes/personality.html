Essay

“LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch”

In this era with the rapid implementation of AI, must it be used as a tool or acrutch? A tool in this instance is a resource that is used to make a task easier or achieve a specific goal (cambridge.org,2026). A crutch is a feature or automated system that a person relies on heavily and that replaces their basic skills, such as critical thinking or problem-solving (Wilson B, 2025). Analysing how AI can be used as a tool versus being used as a crutch by identifying and expounding on instances and my approach to how it can be used to improve and enhance work as a student.


Firstly, AI can be used as a tool to give feedback, explain confusing topics, and brainstorm. In an article ” How to help your students use AI without losing the learning,” students were tasked with using AI in a debate exercise to generate their counterarguments. Those who used it showed improvement in critical thinking and argumentative skills, versus the peers who did not use it. This depicts how AI can be used properly while maintaining academic integrity for feedback.


Secondly, using it to brainstorm and explain confusing topics in the article “Help Students Use AI To Improve Their Research, Not Replace Their Thinking by the University of Michigan guides students on how to help research more in depth and detail by building a stronger foundation and developing their own perspective. When complex topics are given to AI, it can break them down into a basic outline or guide. However, AI becomes a crutch when it is used to cheat and leads to dependence on technology. In this article, “The largest study of AI use by undergrads is in, revealing disparities in access — and in cheating,” it reported that 9% of students at Berkley uses it to cheat and that mostly non-STEM students use it. It becomes alarming when students submit work that is done by AI because they start to think the material given is not important and believe that grades are more important than learning.


In terms of dependency, the article “The Growing Dependency on AI in Academia” notes that students begin to lack critical thinking, academic performance, and ethical integrity. Instances such as using it to generate essays or solve problems. Students begin to doubt their own critical thinking skills.Lastly, how I restrict my usage of AI due to fear of becoming dependent. Mainly, I use it in preparation for tests, to explain complex topics that I do not understand, and to break down mathematical concepts. However, I ensure to do thorough research before and after resorting to AI because it is not accurate. In conclusion, AI can be used as a tool but must not be used as a crutch because it can cause dependency and the information given is not accurate,learning to use your critical skills is important.
